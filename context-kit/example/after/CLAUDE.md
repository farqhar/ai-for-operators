# Project: Harbour Cafe loyalty pilot

6-week loyalty pilot for a fictional cafe group. Goal: decide by week 6
whether to roll the stamp card out to all five stores.

## Read first
- `work/status.md` — where things are this week
- `docs/brief.md` — goals, constraints, success measures

## Read only when asked
- `docs/survey-questions.md` — full survey
- `docs/meeting-notes.md` — past decisions, newest at top

## Never read
- `archive/` — old exports and superseded drafts
- `data/raw/` — POS exports. Use `data/summary.md` instead.

## Rules
- Plain English, short paragraphs.
- Never email customers directly. Draft only.
- When you finish a task, update `work/status.md` in 3 lines or fewer.
